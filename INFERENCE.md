# Native inference

Unconditional margins: coefficient influence plus D_i(delta_i-theta)/M, summed by cluster. CR1 G/(G-1)*(N-1)/(N-rank(A)), Student t(G-1). Includes composition uncertainty. Nonuniform native weights unsupported.

## November 2026 support revision

The Stata delete-cluster calculation now recomputes the estimator on surviving support. Disappearance of an adoption cohort by itself does not invalidate a deletion. Every required deletion must yield an estimate for a jackknife standard error. FLEX compacts each state-year by QR and obtains the untreated projection, treated margins and clustered scores without expanding treatment interactions at the individual level.
